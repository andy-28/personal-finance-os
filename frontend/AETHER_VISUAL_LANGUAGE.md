# Aether Visual Language

Coin Engine is a financial operating system rendered as an arcane workstation. It should feel like a practical daily finance tool whose financial objects have been digitized by a quiet fantasy technology layer.

## Direction

- Fantasy x Arcane Technology x Financial OS.
- Dark steel, obsidian, smoked glass, crystal, aged brass, and controlled cyan energy.
- System UI first, game identity second.
- Strong silhouettes and restrained glow, readable at dashboard density.

## Not The Direction

- Not cyberpunk.
- Not medieval fantasy.
- Not a clone of any existing game UI.
- No MapleStory, Nexon, bank artwork, card artwork, logo, sprite, font, or sound asset.
- No emoji as production placeholders.

## Asset Strength

- Level 1 Utility: search, filter, edit, add. Low contrast and functional.
- Level 2 System: account, ledger, credit card, category. Clear identity without stealing focus.
- Level 3 Resource: coin, asset crystal, debt shard, goal star. Higher visual identity.
- Level 4 Object Art: wallet, card face, goal crystal. Local visual focus only.
- Level 5 Effects: success glow, selection energy. Rare and brief.

## Icon Art Direction

Future custom artwork should use:

- Game inventory icon style.
- Transparent background.
- Front-facing or slight isometric angle.
- Strong silhouette.
- Medium detail.
- Readable at 24-48 px.
- Fantasy arcane technology.
- Dark metallic material.
- Controlled cyan magical energy.
- No text, logo, watermark, or bank branding.

## Material Notes

- Asset: emerald or cyan crystal tone.
- Debt: ruby shard tone.
- Net worth: amber core tone.
- Available credit: cyan or green energy.
- Pending: amber.
- Error: red.

Keep color semantic and token-driven where possible. Do not bake final meaning into one-off component CSS.
